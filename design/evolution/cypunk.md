# Cypunk evolution proposals

These are review proposals only. They are not imported by the live game, included in its catalog or used by saved pets. Every form uses a complete canvas no larger than 12 by 12 pixels. The baby, child, teens and adults include idle, happy, sad, eating and sleeping expressions. The egg has two idle poses.

MissingNumber is an original abstract design, not a copied game sprite. Language marks and error numbers stay readable during care reactions. Matrix advances two accent pixels between idle poses. Glitch Elder shifts one detached pixel. Neither uses flashing or rapid color changes.

## Pathways

Binary Seed becomes Bit, then Byte. At child evolution, at most one care mistake leads to Compiler. Two or more lead to Syntax Error.

Compiler adult routes are evaluated in this order. The first matching condition wins:

| Priority | Requirement at adult evolution | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | At most two care mistakes and 100% discipline | JavaScript |
| 2 | At most two care mistakes and at least 75% discipline | C# |
| 3 | At most four care mistakes and at least 50% discipline | Matrix |
| 4 | At least 50% discipline, with earlier conditions not met | 404 |
| 5 | At least 25% discipline, with earlier conditions not met | MissingNumber |
| 6 | All other cases | Blue Screen |

Syntax Error has three adult routes. At least 75% discipline leads to 404. Otherwise, at least 50% leads to MissingNumber. Below 50% leads to Blue Screen.

Matrix is the special elder route. Once it reaches age 10 or older with 100% discipline, each eligible simulation tick has a 5% chance to become Glitch Elder. It must still be alive. No other adult takes this route. This is the same path as the current cracked Lilgotchi egg, and does not introduce new care rules.

## Review questions

Check that JS, C# and 404 remain legible at the actual game scale. Compare the three streams of Matrix against the irregular silhouette of MissingNumber. Keep Glitch Elder visibly older and more fragmented than Syntax Error. The preview should show all six expressions so the names are not the only distinguishing feature.
