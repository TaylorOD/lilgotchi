# Cypunk evolution paths

These paths and sprites ship in lilgotchi 1.0.0. The preview reads the same artwork modules as the live game. Each type covers the original evolution branches while retaining its own appearance.

## Lineup

| Original identity | Name | Shape and detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| egg | Binary Seed | Rounded cyan seed carrying tiny binary marks |
| babytchi | Bit | Small digital block with a face and two feet |
| marutchi | Byte | Wider chip with an antenna and side contacts |
| tamatchi | Compiler | Upright terminal character with a neat head and body |
| kuchitamatchi | Syntax Error | Uneven terminal with detached magenta pixels |
| mametchi | JavaScript | Cyan robot with yellow antenna tips, scarf, hands and feet |
| ginjirotchi | Sentinel | The original Cypunk adult, with its exact sprite and expressions |
| maskutchi | Matrix | Green robot with antennae, a face and falling torso lights |
| kuchipatchi | 404 | Lost little robot with a bent magenta antenna and lowered arms |
| nyorotchi | MissingNumber | Original asymmetric block creature with broken edges and magenta fragments |
| tarakotchi | Blue Screen | Wide blue monitor companion with a face, arms and feet |
| oyajitchi | Glitch Elder | Cyan elder made of separated chunks and magenta fragments |

MissingNumber is an original abstract design, not a copied game sprite. Faces stay readable during care reactions. Matrix advances two accent pixels between idle poses. Glitch Elder shifts one detached pixel. Neither uses flashing or rapid color changes.

## Pathways

Binary Seed becomes Bit, then Byte. At child evolution, at most one care mistake leads to Compiler. Two or more lead to Syntax Error.

Compiler adult routes are evaluated in this order. The first matching condition wins:

| Priority | Requirement at adult evolution | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | At most two care mistakes and 100% discipline | JavaScript |
| 2 | At most two care mistakes and at least 75% discipline | Sentinel |
| 3 | At most four care mistakes and at least 50% discipline | Matrix |
| 4 | At least 50% discipline, with earlier conditions not met | 404 |
| 5 | At least 25% discipline, with earlier conditions not met | MissingNumber |
| 6 | All other cases | Blue Screen |

Syntax Error has three adult routes. At least 75% discipline leads to 404. Otherwise, at least 50% leads to MissingNumber. Below 50% leads to Blue Screen.

Matrix is the special elder route. Once it reaches age 10 or older with 100% discipline, each eligible simulation tick has a 5% chance to become Glitch Elder. It must still be alive. No other adult takes this route. This is the same path as the current cracked Lilgotchi egg, and does not introduce new care rules.

## Review questions

Check that JavaScript, Sentinel and 404 feel like individual companions at the actual game scale. Compare the green robot Matrix against the irregular silhouette of MissingNumber. Keep Glitch Elder visibly older and more fragmented than Syntax Error. The preview should show all six expressions so the names are not the only distinguishing feature.


## Character revision

The C# badge is replaced by Sentinel, the exact original Cypunk adult sprite and expressions. Sentinel occupies the Ginjirotchi branch in this lineup. JavaScript is a friendly cyan robot with yellow antenna tips and a yellow scarf, not a lettering tile. 404 is a lost little robot with a bent magenta antenna and lowered arms, keeping the original Cypunk proportions. Neither wears a word or number across its face.

Matrix is a green robot with antennae, a bright face, hands and feet. Small falling code lights travel down its torso. It has no hood. Binary Seed keeps the original readable 0 and 1 in both egg poses. Blue Screen is a wider monitor companion with a face, little arms and sturdy feet. MissingNumber and Glitch Elder retain their reviewed shapes.
